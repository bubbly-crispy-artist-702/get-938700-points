# get 938700 points without getting hit 🔥

This is my MakeCode Arcade game! You fly a little guy around (he's on fire,
which looks awesome) and gray dudes float up from the bottom of the screen
and you have to NOT touch them. Your score goes up super fast just for
staying alive — if you survive long enough, you win! You get 3 lives, and
every gray dude that hits you takes one.

Fun fact: the title says 938,700 but the code actually lets you win at
938,000. That's 700 free points. You're welcome. 😎

## 🛠️ How it works

* Your score goes up by 900 every single frame just for being alive
* A new gray dude spawns every 340 milliseconds at a random spot at the bottom and floats up
* If one touches you: he pops, you lose a life
* Lives hit zero = game over. Score hits 938,000 = YOU WIN
* My guy has a 3-frame animation and a fire effect, and I scaled him up to 238% so you can see him better
